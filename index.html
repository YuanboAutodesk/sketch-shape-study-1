Which 3D shape matches the sketch better?

Each round shows a hand sketch and two 3D shapes built from it, A and B. You pick the better one — four times: once for each of three things, and once overall.

1. Details
How much of the detail in the sketch is actually modelled — handles, grooves, buttons, holes, chamfers. Is the shape as richly detailed as the drawing?
2. Proportion
Whether the parts have the right relative sizes and are in the right places — is anything too tall, too fat, too small, or attached in the wrong spot?
3. Fidelity
How faithfully the shape as a whole depicts the object that was sketched, and whether it reads as a real, buildable object.
4. Overall
All things considered, which is the better result for this sketch?

Four choices per round: details, proportion, fidelity, overall. They do not have to agree — one shape can win on detail and lose on proportion.

  • Drag to rotate a shape, scroll to zoom. Each shape turns on its own — moving one never moves the other.
  • There is no right answer and no time limit. If the two look equally good on something, pick whichever you would rather have.
  • 20 shapes to get through, drawn at random from the 24 in the study — no object comes up twice. Which of the two is on which side changes every time.

Before you start

Pick a username. It is the only thing identifying you, and it names the results file you send back — so use something you will recognise, and the same one if you ever run this twice. No email address is asked for.
